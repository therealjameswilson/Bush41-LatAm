FRUS Method

Compiler-Facing Review Points

Selection Standard

Flag records that document major policy decisions, significant diplomatic activity, and high-level decision pathways for the South America chapter sequence.

Chronology Rule

Memcons and telcons are sorted by the date of the conversation, matching the FRUS practice of arranging documents by Washington, DC time.

Source Note Model

Citations follow the Volume XXXI Bush-document pattern: repository, Bush Presidential Records, office or collection, series, OA/ID or file unit, folder title, then release facts. Scowcroft citations are drawn from the PDF provenance sheet.

Diary/Backup Use

Presidential Daily Diary and Daily Backup files are treated as schedule and call-reference evidence. They help identify times, participants, and supporting materials but are kept separate from substantive memcon and telcon texts.

Declassification Check

Partial releases, local page-range extracts, and catalog-only provenance are exposed as review cues for later comparison with restricted or newly opened files.

Method anchor: Office of the Historian, About the Foreign Relations Series.
