A companion archive for declassified memoranda and telephone conversations relevant to Foreign Relations of the United States, 1989-1992, Volume XXV, Latin America. Central America is intentionally excluded because it has its own FRUS volume. This site arranges the South America file country by country, with each chapter ordered chronologically.
